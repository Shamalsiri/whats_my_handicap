# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 12/29/2024

Welcome to **"What's My Handicap"** ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and store your information when you use our golf tracking app (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect and store the following types of information locally on your device:

- **Personal Information**: During registration, we collect your first and last name, date of birth, email, and password.
- **Golf Round Data**: When you play a round of golf, we store information such as the club name, course name, number of holes, slope rating, and the date and time the round is started.
- **Device Date and Time**: The app uses the device’s current date and time to record your golf rounds.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

- To provide core functionality, including tracking your scores, calculating your handicap, and maintaining a history of your golf rounds.
- To allow you to edit and manage your golf round data.

**Note**: All data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with external servers or third-party services.

---

## 3. Data Retention
Your data is stored locally on your device and remains accessible until:

- You delete the app.
- You clear the app's data from your device.

---

## 4. Security
We take reasonable precautions to protect the information stored locally on your device. However, the security of your device is your responsibility, and we recommend taking measures such as using a secure password and avoiding unauthorized access.

---

## 5. Age Restrictions
The app is intended for users aged 5 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from users under the age of 5.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. Changes will be communicated with an updated **"Effective Date."**
